Lead Cleanup

The goal is simple: fewer dropped leads, faster replies, cleaner quote follow-up, and less relying on memory.

01

Catch the lead before they call someone else.

Most customers do not wait around forever. A simple response path can keep the conversation alive while you are working.

  • Missed-call text back: fast messages that let customers know you saw the call and will follow up.
  • Quote requests: forms and reminders that keep new work from getting buried.
  • Lead tracking: a clear place to see who needs a call, text, quote, or next step.
02

Follow up without turning into a pest.

Good follow-up is useful, timely, and tied to the work. It should help the customer take the next step without feeling spammy.

  • Estimate nudges: simple reminders for quotes that have gone quiet.
  • Review requests: clean messages after finished work, when the customer is most likely to respond.
  • Customer check-ins: repeat-work and referral follow-up that still sounds like a real business.
