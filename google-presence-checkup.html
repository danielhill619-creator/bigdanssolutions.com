What I Check

The goal is not to chase every keyword. The goal is to remove the obvious reasons customers pick somebody else.

A business can lose the call because the listing looks unfinished, the website does not explain the service, the phone path is weak, or the profile does not show enough proof.

01

Google Business Profile

I look for the basics that help a local business look current, real, and easy to contact.

  • Profile details: category, services, hours, phone, website, and service area.
  • Trust signals: logo, cover image, real business photos, posts, and review path.
  • Conversion path: whether a customer can call, click, message, or request help without confusion.
02

Website and search basics

A Google listing works better when the website backs it up with clear service pages and a simple contact path.

  • Service clarity: whether the site says what you do in customer language.
  • Local relevance: whether Google and customers can see where you work.
  • Next step: whether the visitor knows what to do after reading.
03

First fix recommendation

The checkup should produce one practical first move, not a giant report that never gets used.

  • Fast cleanup: the obvious fix that helps the profile or site look more hire-ready.
  • Follow-up gap: whether calls, forms, texts, or quote requests are easy to miss.
  • Bigger opportunity: where a simple system, page, or automation would help next.
