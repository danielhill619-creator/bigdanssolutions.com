01

Get Found

When customers search, compare, or ask around, the business should look clear, current, and easy to contact.

  • Websites and online presence: clear pages, cleaner messaging, service areas, calls to action, and contact paths.
  • Google Business Profile: profile cleanup, service details, photos, posts, categories, and trust signals.
  • Local visibility: service pages, location signals, reviews, and clearer contact paths.

Start with a Workflow Checkup before spending money on more traffic.

02

Catch the Lead

Most lost business does not announce itself. It leaks out through missed calls, slow replies, forgotten quotes, and follow-up that depends on memory.

  • Missed-call text back: fast response paths so customers do not move on while you are on a job.
  • Follow-up and reminders: quote nudges, appointment messages, review requests, and customer check-ins.
  • Customer tracking: simple CRM cleanup so leads, customers, referrals, and repeat work do not get lost.

Start with a Workflow Checkup to see where good leads are slipping through.

03

Clean Up the Work

If the same details get typed three times, quotes take too long, or nobody knows where the next step lives, the workflow is costing more than it looks like.

  • Scheduling and job tracking: clearer handoffs, next steps, documents, notes, and closeout.
  • Estimating and paperwork: cleaner quote building, approvals, records, owner questions, and customer-ready output.
  • Dashboards, reporting, and automation: simple owner views and repeat tasks built around the real workflow.
