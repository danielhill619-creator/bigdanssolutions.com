## Outreach Rule

Do not spam. Pick businesses where a real, visible issue exists and send a short, specific message. The first message should point to one practical thing, not a generic pitch.

## Best Search Targets

Start with service businesses where missed calls, quotes, reviews, Google profile quality, and website clarity directly affect revenue:

- Roofers and exterior contractors
- HVAC and electrical contractors
- Plumbers and septic contractors
- Home repair and renovation contractors

## What To Look For

Good prospects usually show at least two of these:

- Google profile has few or no posts.
- Profile has weak or missing services.
- Logo or cover photo is missing or low quality.
- Website is outdated, thin, slow, confusing, or missing service-area detail.
- Contact path is unclear.
- No obvious missed-call or quote follow-up process.
- Reviews exist but there is no visible review request system.
- Business is active but online presence looks neglected.
