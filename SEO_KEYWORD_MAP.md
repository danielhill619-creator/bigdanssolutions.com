## Rules

- One page gets one primary search intent.
- Supporting phrases belong only where they read naturally.
- Do not target work Big Dan's Solutions does not want, such as tax, loan, legal, or accounting consulting.
- Use "near me" sparingly. Google mostly resolves that through Google Business Profile, service area, local page wording, reviews, and prominence.
