Put customer work in one cleaner flow.

CRM setup should make the business easier to run, not create a new place where details go to die.

  • Customer tracking: names, notes, jobs, conversations, documents, and next steps in a usable structure.
  • Lead and quote follow-up: reminders so calls, forms, estimates, and approvals do not disappear.
  • Owner view: simple reporting that shows who needs attention without checking five tools.
02

Automate the repeat work that keeps stealing time.

Automation works when it supports the real process. It should reduce admin work while still leaving judgment with the owner.

  • Reminders and routing: quote nudges, review requests, appointment reminders, and cleanup queues.
  • Workflow cleanup: fewer duplicate entries, clearer handoffs, and better follow-through.
  • Practical tools: spreadsheets, CRM platforms, private dashboards, or custom screens when that is the right fit.
