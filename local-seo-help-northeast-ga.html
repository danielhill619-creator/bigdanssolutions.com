Free First Moves

A lot of getting found on Google is not magic. It is accurate details, useful pages, real photos, honest reviews, and steady cleanup.

01

Clean up the Google side.

Google needs a clear, current business profile and a website it can crawl without confusion.

  • Business Profile: services, hours, service areas, photos, updates, and review links.
  • Search Console: sitemap checks, indexing requests, performance data, and page issues.
  • Analytics: tracking that shows whether people are actually landing on the site.
02

Build pages around what owners and customers actually search.

One homepage usually cannot carry every search. Focused local and service pages give Google more specific answers.

  • Service-area wording: practical references to Toccoa, Stephens County, and Northeast Georgia.
  • Service pages: website help, help getting found on Google, follow-up, automation, and workflow cleanup.
  • Trust signals: photos, examples, reviews, contact details, and plain proof that the business is real.
